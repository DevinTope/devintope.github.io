Ball Stattracker

02

Less time tapping. More time in the game. Mobile softball scorekeeping with automatic runner advancement and season batting statistics.

  • Next.js
  • TypeScript
  • Prisma
  • Zustand
Behind the project +

Record plate appearances, review runner movements, and track a team’s season. Zustand manages game state while PostgreSQL and Prisma store teams, games, and statistics.

The focus: keeping the scorekeeping workflow quick and comfortable on a phone.
